    Exciting Escapes  

    Jeep Safari (On Request)

    Unleash Adventure, Discover Nature

    Embark on a thrilling Jeep safari in Wagamon, starting at the stunning Wagamon View Point for panoramic hill and tea plantation views. Explore the intriguing Vellaramchitta Tunnel, enjoy scenic dam views, and experience an adrenaline-filled off-road ride through rugged terrain. It's an unforgettable adventure in Wagamon's natural beauty.

    Paragliding (Seasonal)

    Soar High with Wagamon Paragliding

    Wagamon's paragliding experience is a breathtaking adventure that takes you soaring high above the lush landscapes of Kerala. Glide through the skies, feeling the rush of wind as you admire panoramic views of tea plantations, rolling hills, and serene lakes below. With experienced instructors and top-notch equipment, Wagamon offers a safe and exhilarating paragliding experience for thrill-seekers and nature lovers alike. It's a truly unforgettable way to experience the beauty of Wagamon from a whole new perspective.

    Hiking

    Vagamon's Windy Walk Trek

    Explore the scenic Vakavanam trail, known as Vagamon Windy Walk Trekking, through grasslands and shola forests. Enjoy stunning views of Idukki reservoir's catchment area and vast mountain ranges. Keep an eye out for wildlife like elephants. It's a serene and rewarding hike in Wagamon

    *Extra Charges Apply.
