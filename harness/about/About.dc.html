OUR STORY

Rooted in the Mountains, Gathered Around the Table

A small resort with a simple idea: that a beautiful place, honest food and genuine care can turn a few days away into something you remember for years.

HOW IT BEGAN

It Started With One Long Table and a View

The Mountain Table was born from evenings spent outdoors with friends and family — a long wooden table, food from nearby farms and the sun sinking behind the ridges. We wanted to share that feeling with every guest who finds their way up the mountain.

Since [YEAR], we have grown from a handful of rooms into a quiet retreat, but the heart of it hasn't changed: slow mornings, good company and a table that always has room for one more.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

The Values That Guide Every Stay

Respect the Land

We build lightly, care for the forest around us and keep the mountain as wild as we found it.

Local First

Our kitchen works with nearby farmers, foragers and makers, so every plate tastes of this place.

Unhurried Hospitality

We know our guests by name and take the time to get the small things right.

Made by Hand

Stone, timber and craft from the region shape our rooms, furniture and the details in between.

We don’t want guests to simply visit the mountains. We want them to slow down long enough to feel at home in them.

— THE MOUNTAIN TABLE FAMILY
